## What This Project Is

A full Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity Plan developed for a healthcare IT environment. Healthcare is one of the most regulated and targeted sectors in cybersecurity. This plan covers everything from risk assessment and business impact analysis to recovery strategies and testing procedures.

This is the kind of document GRC analysts, risk managers, and IT security teams produce when preparing an organization to survive and recover from a major incident, whether that is a ransomware attack, a natural disaster, or a critical system failure.

## What the Plan Covers

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk Assessment | Identified threats, vulnerabilities, and likelihood of impact across healthcare IT systems |
| Business Impact Analysis | Determined critical systems, dependencies, and the financial and operational impact of downtime |
| Emergency Response Plan | Step by step actions to take immediately when a disaster occurs |
| Communication Plan | Who gets notified, how, and in what order during an incident |
| Recovery Strategies | Technical and procedural steps to restore systems and operations |
| IT Infrastructure Inventory | Full inventory of systems, hardware, and software covered by the plan |
| Data Backup and Restoration Plan | Backup schedules, retention policies, and step by step restoration procedures |
| Resource Inventory | Personnel, equipment, and vendor contacts needed during recovery |
| Training and Awareness | How staff are trained on DR/BCP procedures |
| Testing and Maintenance | How and when the plan is tested and updated |

## Key Terms

**RTO (Recovery Time Objective):** The maximum acceptable time to restore a system after a disaster. For critical healthcare systems this is measured in hours, not days.

**RPO (Recovery Point Objective):** The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time. For patient records this could be as low as 15 minutes.

**MTD (Maximum Tolerable Downtime):** The absolute longest a system can be down before the impact becomes unacceptable. This drives the RTO requirement.

**BIA (Business Impact Analysis):** The process of identifying which systems are critical and what happens if they go down. This forms the foundation of the entire plan.

## Why Healthcare

Healthcare IT is one of the most attacked sectors and one of the most regulated. HIPAA requires covered entities to have contingency plans covering data backup, disaster recovery, and emergency operations. A DR/BCP failure in healthcare can directly impact patient safety, which makes this a high stakes environment to plan for.
