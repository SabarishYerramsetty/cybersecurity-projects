## What This Project Is

A set of enterprise-grade security policies, standards, and procedures developed as part of the cybersecurity program at UCM. These are the kinds of documents a GRC analyst, security architect, or compliance officer produces to govern how an organization handles its information assets, systems, and data.

Policy writing is a core skill in GRC, IAM, and risk roles. Having actual written policies demonstrates you understand not just the technical side of security but the governance and process side as well.

---

## Documents Included

| Document | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Acceptable Use Policy | Policy | Defines how employees are permitted to use organizational IT assets, systems, and data. Sets boundaries for acceptable behavior and consequences for violations. |
| Asset Management Policy | Policy | Governs how the organization identifies, classifies, tracks, and manages its information assets throughout their lifecycle. |
| Logging Standard | Standard | Defines the minimum requirements for what must be logged across systems, how long logs must be retained, and how they must be protected. |
| Information Disposal Standard | Standard | Specifies how information and assets must be securely disposed of at end of life to prevent unauthorized data recovery. |
| Logging Procedure | Procedure | Step by step instructions for implementing the Logging Standard. Covers how to configure log collection, storage, and monitoring. |
| Information Disposal Procedure | Procedure | Step by step instructions for securely disposing of physical and digital information assets in line with the Information Disposal Standard. |

---

## Policy vs Standard vs Procedure

**Policy** is the "what and why." It sets the organizational intent and requirement at a high level. Approved by leadership. Example: "All company data must be classified and handled according to its classification level."

**Standard** is the "how much." It defines the specific technical or process requirements that must be met to comply with the policy. Example: "All logs must be retained for a minimum of 12 months."

**Procedure** is the "how to." Step by step instructions for carrying out the standard. Written for the people who do the work. Example: "To configure log retention, navigate to Settings, then Log Management, then Retention Policy and set the value to 365 days."

---

## Why This Matters for SOC and GRC Roles

SOC analysts work within the boundaries set by policies and standards every day. Knowing how these documents are structured and why they exist makes you a better analyst because you understand the governance context behind the technical controls you are implementing.

For GRC roles, the ability to write clear and enforceable policy documents is a direct job requirement.
